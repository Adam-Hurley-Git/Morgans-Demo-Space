02 — Web Application

Event Manager App

A full-stack coordination tool built to bring event briefings, timelines, and team coordination into one structured, real-time workspace.

The Problem

Morgans hosts complex events — weddings, corporate dinners, private parties — each with unique layouts, staffing, and detailed timelines. Keeping every team member fully briefed, with the right information to hand at the right moment during a live shift, is no small task.

The Solution

A purpose-built web app that gives event managers a structured workspace to organise events from the top down, and gives front-of-house staff a clean mobile interface to stay coordinated — without needing to scroll through chat threads to find their briefing.

Admin · Desktop

Event Manager Dashboard

The admin panel is designed for event managers working at a desk. They can create events, upload the PDF briefings they already use, build out a digital timeline, and assign staff to specific roles and rooms — all in one place.

  • ✦ Create and manage multiple concurrent events
  • ✦ Upload existing PDF briefings — brief is digitised into the app
  • ✦ Build timelines with setup tasks, timings, and checkpoints
  • ✦ Assign individual staff to rooms and roles
  • ✦ Full event overview at a glance, no searching required
Front of House · Mobile

Staff View

Staff access the app on their phones or company iPads. They see exactly what's relevant to their role: their timeline, their tasks, and where they're assigned. Tasks are ticked off in real time as setup progresses through the shift.

  • ✦ Personal timeline view — only what's relevant to that staff member
  • ✦ Tap to tick off setup tasks as the event takes shape
  • ✦ Room and role assignment visible at a glance
  • ✦ Works on any phone or iPad — no app download required
  • ✦ Updates in real time as admin makes changes

This is a working demonstration build. All features are fully functional.
